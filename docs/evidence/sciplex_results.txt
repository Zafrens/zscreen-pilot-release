# Direct public SciPlex3 comparison

All 83 held-out drug–dose–cell conditions are scored on the same 2,000-gene axis with equal condition weights. They represent nine unseen drugs and 18,697 measured cells. Released scBalFlow and chemCPA predictions are actual inference from their supplied checkpoints; the two conditional Chemprop arms use identical new public supervision and three paired seeds.

## Matched Z-Screen pretraining effect

Differences below pair the same condition and training seed; positive R²/correlation differences and negative error differences favor Z-Screen pretraining. Seed SD describes model-run variability, not a confidence interval.

Response correlation uses predicted and observed changes from the same all-training-control mean. The population-control baseline has no predicted change, so its response correlation is undefined. Response RMSE/MAE equal the corresponding absolute-profile errors because the same control is subtracted from both vectors. Negative R² values are retained.

The released models have their authors’ original training histories. Their comparison establishes external reference performance on this public panel; the matched Chemprop comparison estimates the contribution of Z-Screen pretraining under fixed public supervision. These results evaluate condition means, not full single-cell distributions.

Per-cell, per-drug, per-seed and per-condition values, training labels and paired effects are saved beside this report. Nine drugs underlie the 83 related cell/dose contexts; no claim of 83 independent chemical samples or statistical significance is made.
